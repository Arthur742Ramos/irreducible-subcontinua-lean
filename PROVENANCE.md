Paul Bankston's [Metric Topology: A First Course](https://www.mscsnet.mu.edu/~paul/Paper/4450102text.pdf#page=89), course notes for Math 4450, Spring 2011, is the primary proof source. Proposition 28.1 states existence of a subcontinuum irreducible about a nonempty subset of a Hausdorff continuum. The formal statement treats the ambient continuum as a compact connected subset C of a Hausdorff space X. The minimality clause is restricted to compact connected subsets L of the chosen K that contain A. Proposition 27.1 supports the intersection proof.

The intersection theorem covers a nonempty family directed downward under inclusion, with compact connected members. Its intersection is nonempty and connected. The existence proof applies this result to chains of candidates, then uses Mathlib's `zorn_superset_nonempty` and `Minimal.eq_of_subset`. The two-point theorem specializes A to `{x, y}` and allows x = y.
